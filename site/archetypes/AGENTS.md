# Content archetypes

## Purpose

Define minimal front matter and scaffolding for new Hugo content.

## Rules

- Archetypes initialize files; they do not render the website or store existing content.
- Keep new content draft by default unless an approved workflow says otherwise.
- Do not invent canonical facts or reusable fixed node IDs in templates.
- Keep Hugo publishing fields separate from future root node: metadata.
- Do not expand the ontology or prescribe a graph schema without approval.
